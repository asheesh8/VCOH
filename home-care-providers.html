Now recruiting

Open your home. Change a life.

Currently, we are recruiting for home care providers. We are seeking reliable, caring individuals willing to share their home and change the life of someone in their community.

You'll have the support of an Agency, a Care Coordinator, and needed respite — plus a very generous stipend, room-and-board monthly payments, and any additional training you need.

Become a provider
New in Vermont

Now paying family caregivers. Vermont now allows payments to adult children caring for their parents. All current eligibility criteria and regulations apply.

Provider responsibilities

  • 24-hour support and supervision.
  • Assistance with personal care and medication management.
  • Meal preparation.
  • Scheduling appointments and transportation.

Qualifications & requirements

  • Reliable, caring, responsible people who want to make a difference.
  • Criminal background check.
  • Home safety inspection.
  • Maintain both homeowners and motor-vehicle insurance.

Our skilled Care Coordinators provide regular and ongoing support to both the client and the home care provider.
