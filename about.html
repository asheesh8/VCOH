Who are we?

A choice, when living at home is no longer possible

The Vermont Comforts of Home program is jointly marketed by two Vermont organizations: Lincoln Street, Inc. and Upper Valley Services, Inc.

The goal of Vermont Comforts of Home is to provide a choice when, due to medical and personal-care needs, a person can no longer live at home without support.

What are our values?

All people, regardless of their age or care needs, should have a voice in how they are supported and where they live.

What services do we provide?

  • Care coordination
  • Residential support
  • Safety

Services operate in compliance with the regulations of the Adult Family Care program under Vermont's Choices for Care initiative.
