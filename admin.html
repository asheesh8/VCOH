How this works

Everything on the site can be changed from here without opening a code editor.

Editing text

  • Open the Pages tab and pick a page.
  • Hover — anything with a dashed green outline can be edited. Click it and type.
  • The floating toolbar gives you bold, italic, underline, links, text size, alignment and brand colours.
  • Select some words first to format just those words; with nothing selected, colour and size apply to the whole block.
  • Esc finishes editing. The circular arrow on the toolbar undoes every unpublished change to that one block.

Adding and removing items

  • Bullet points, cards, office boxes and other repeated items show a duplicate and a delete button on the toolbar.
  • Duplicate makes a copy directly below — then click the copy and type over it.

Photos

  • Click any photo to replace it, either by choosing a file or pasting an image address.
  • Uploaded photos are stored inside the content file, so keep them small (under about 500 KB). For anything larger, add the file to assets/images and point at it by name.

Staff

  • The Staff tab manages the whole About-page team list — organisations, role groups and people.
  • Initials on the round badges are worked out from each name automatically.
  • The team section can't be edited from the Pages tab, because it is built from this list.

Getting back here

Triple-click the logo/banner in the footer of any page. There is no password — anyone who knows the trick can edit, so keep it to yourself.

Changes are saved in this browser. They will not appear for visitors, and they will not follow you to another computer, until you publish (see the Publish tab).

Clearing your browser data clears unpublished drafts, so publish when you are happy.
