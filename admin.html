How this works

Everything on the site can be changed from here without opening a code editor.

Editing text

  • Open the Pages tab and pick a page. Use English / नेपाली above the page to choose which language you are editing.
  • Hover — anything with a dashed green outline can be edited. Click it and type.
  • The floating toolbar gives you bold, italic, underline, links, text size, alignment and brand colours.
  • Select some words first to format just those words; with nothing selected, colour and size apply to the whole block.
  • Esc finishes editing. The circular arrow on the toolbar undoes every unpublished change to that one block.

Adding and removing items

  • Bullet points, cards, office boxes and other repeated items show a duplicate and a delete button on the toolbar.
  • Duplicate makes a copy directly below — then click the copy and type over it.

Photos

  • Click any photo to replace it, either by choosing a file or pasting an image address.
  • Uploaded photos are stored with the site (up to 3 MB each) and appear on the live site after you publish. The same photo shows in both languages.

Nepali (नेपाली)

  • Every page has a Nepali version that visitors can switch to at the top right of the site.
  • Switch the editor to नेपाली and click any text to change the Nepali wording. English and Nepali are saved separately.
  • If you change English text, its old Nepali translation no longer matches, so that block shows in English on the Nepali site until you add the Nepali for it.
  • On the Staff tab, each group and job title has an optional Nepali box.

Staff

  • The Staff tab manages the whole About-page team list — organisations, role groups and people.
  • Initials on the round badges are worked out from each name automatically.
  • The team section can't be edited from the Pages tab, because it is built from this list.

Getting back here

Triple-click the logo/banner in the footer of any page, then enter the passcode. Press Sign out when you are done on a shared computer.

Edits are kept in this browser until you press Save & publish. Visitors see nothing until then, and unsaved edits do not follow you to another computer.

Clearing your browser data clears unsaved edits, so publish when you are happy.
