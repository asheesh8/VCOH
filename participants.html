Care coordination

A plan built around you

  • Create an individualized plan of care.
  • Schedule and coordinate medical care and appointments.
  • Ensure safe medication management.
  • Match individuals with caring and qualified Home Care Providers in the home community.
  • Connect participants to resources and needed supports in the greater community.
Shared living

Just the right home for you

When you are no longer able to live in your own home, one of our skilled home care providers may offer just the right home for you. Our goal is to tailor services to the unique needs of each participant in the Vermont Comforts of Home program.

Good to know

Qualifying, cost & placement

Who qualifies?

If you are 18 or older and require a nursing-home level of care — extensive assistance with medical care, personal care, taking medications, mobility, decision-making, or safely accessing the community — you may qualify.

What does it cost?

$826.65/mo

Most participants are eligible for Medicaid, which pays for most of these services. Participants contribute approximately $826.65 per month toward room & board.

How are people placed?

The key to this program's success is finding the right match of participant and provider. There are never more than two participants in one person's home.
