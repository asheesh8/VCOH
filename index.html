About the program

What is Vermont Comforts of Home?

Vermont Comforts of Home is a non-profit, collaborative program managed and implemented by Lincoln Street, Inc. and Upper Valley Services, Inc. Both agencies have significant experience providing high-quality, individualized home care.

Operating in compliance with the Adult Family Care program under Vermont's Choices for Care initiative, we develop community-based options of care for people requiring a nursing-home level of support. Once you contact us, we'll help determine the services needed and which of our two organizations can best serve you.

Eligibility

Who does Vermont Comforts of Home serve?

Vermont residents.
Those who qualify for Medicaid, as well as those interested in a private-pay option.
Those who are 18 years of age or older and require extensive assistance with any of the following: medical care, personal care, taking medications, mobility, decision-making, or safely accessing the community.

How can I find out more?

Our offices are in several locations across Vermont, and we work to identify the perfect match — in providers and location — for those we serve. Please contact us to learn how we might help you stay in the community, or if you're interested in becoming a home care provider.
