Who we are

At some point in our lives, we all need a helping hand. Vermont Comforts of Home believes home is where wellness begins.

Vermont Comforts of Home is a non-profit, collaborative program managed and implemented by Lincoln Street, Inc. and Upper Valley Services, Inc. — operating under Vermont's Choices for Care initiative.

Many people who require a nursing-home level of care prefer to keep living in their community. We are dedicated to providing quality, individualized services that make community living possible for the people we serve.

Our programs

Two ways to be part of the story

Whether you need support to live well in your community — or you're ready to open your home and change a life — there's a place for you here.

Participant services

Community living, made possible

We are social workers, nurses, psychologists and educators with over 40 years of experience supporting individuals with disabilities to live in the community.

When you are no longer able to live in your own home, one of our skilled home care providers may offer just the right home for you — with the support you need to keep living life on your terms.

Care Coordination

  • Create an individualized plan of care
  • Schedule & coordinate medical care and appointments
  • Ensure safe medication management
  • Match you with caring, qualified Home Care Providers in your home community
  • Connect you to resources & supports in the greater community

Shared Living

When living in your own home is no longer possible, a skilled provider opens their home to you. There are never more than two participants in any one provider's home — the right match, not a crowd.

Who qualifies?

If you are 18 or older and require a nursing-home level of care — extensive assistance with medical care, personal care, medications, mobility, decision-making, or safely accessing the community — you may qualify.

$826.65/mo

What does it cost? Most of our participants are eligible for Medicaid, which pays for most of these services. Participants contribute approximately $826.65 per month toward room & board.

The match matters

How are participants placed? The key to this program's success is finding the right match of participant and provider. We currently serve Chittenden, Orange, Addison, Washington, Windham, Windsor and Caledonia counties. Reach out for more information →

Now recruiting

Open your home. Change a life.

We are seeking reliable, caring individuals willing to share their home and change the life of someone in their community.

We identify skilled people committed to providing the support that keeps individuals living in the community — and we back you every step of the way with the support of an Agency, a Care Coordinator, and needed respite.

Become a provider
New in Vermont

Now paying family caregivers. Vermont now allows payments to adult children caring for their parents. All current eligibility criteria and regulations apply.

Provider responsibilities

  • 24-hour support and supervision
  • Assistance with personal care & medication management
  • Meal preparation
  • Scheduling appointments & transportation

Qualifications & requirements

  • Reliable, caring, responsible people who want to make a difference
  • Criminal background check
  • Home safety inspection
  • Maintain homeowners & motor vehicle insurance

What you receive

  • A very generous stipend
  • Monthly room & board payments
  • Any additional training you need
  • Ongoing support from a skilled Care Coordinator

About us

A choice, when living at home is no longer possible

The goal of Vermont Comforts of Home is to provide a choice when, due to medical and personal care needs, a person can no longer live at home without support. The program is jointly marketed by two Vermont organizations — Lincoln Street, Inc. and Upper Valley Services, Inc.

Every voice matters

All people, regardless of age or care needs, should have a voice in how they are supported and where they live.

What we provide

Care coordination, residential support and safety — in compliance with the Adult Family Care program under Vermont's Choices for Care.
